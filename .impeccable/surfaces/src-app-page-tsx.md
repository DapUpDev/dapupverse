# Landing page (src/app/page.tsx)

Scope and mode: the public landing page. Persuade.

Audience and job: a high-school student on AP, IB or A Levels at a laptop, essays open in other tabs, deciding whether a student who got in can help them. Action: name what they need and ask one mentor. Proof: real mentor profiles with photos and universities, and counts read live from the directory. Constraints: frontend only; the headline stays; no invented proof; not corporate, not flashy.

Memorable moment: changing a blank in the sentence slides the matching mentor's card to the front of the stack.

Unresolved: the directory does not yet read the sentence's choices from the URL, so "Browse all mentors" opens it unfiltered.

## Direction contract

THESIS: DapUp is the desk a mentor sits down at: the visitor's request, the mentors' cards and the next step lie on one calm surface. It refuses the tutoring-site arrangement of headline, button and a row of equal tutor cards with ratings.

OWN-WORLD: A cool pale desk (#eceef2). White paper sheets with a small radius and one soft lifted shadow. Graphite ink (#16181d). One highlighter (#d8f04a) that only marks what is chosen, matched or done. Bricolage Grotesque for headlines, Geist for everything read or operated. State is a drawn mark (pencil, dashed ring, ticked ring), never colour alone. Motion is one critically damped ease from where things already are.

STORY: The visitor understands that mentors are real students who got in, believes it from real faces and universities, names what they need in one sentence, and asks one mentor.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, one large sheet: the headline at display scale with "already made it." highlighted, the lede, then a fill-in sentence with three highlighted blanks (exam system, kind of help, university). Right, overlapping the sheet's edge, the real mentors' cards in a stack; the match slides to the front and carries the primary action, "Ask <first name>". A quiet line of live counts closes the sheet.

FORM: The study desk, candidate 5 of 7 grounded directions; seed key 5678e24b; code-led.
